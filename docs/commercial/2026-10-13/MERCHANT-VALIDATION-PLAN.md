# Merchant Validation Plan

Prepared 2026-10-13. Proposed experiment only; **no outreach or merchant work has occurred**. Recommendation: GO_TO_VALIDATE, as defined in [CEO Brief](CEO-BRIEF.md). #214 remains BLOCKED; neither this plan nor a positive interview authorizes implementation.

## One concrete experiment

Start with one consenting WooCommerce reseller or its accountable agency. They must have two successive real supplier CSV cost files, stable SKU identifiers and an upcoming update. The operator and budget owner may differ: record both. Fit the current simple/variation, base-currency boundary and no more than 1,000 selected targets per eventual core job; do not imply a new catalog-size certification.

Ask to see the current workflow before describing WriteLeash. Use voluntarily redacted files/catalog export and screen-sharing evidence, not production credentials. No contact list scraping, trial signup, service purchase or production writes. Founder separately authorizes invitations and any future paid offer. A first merchant is a cheap falsification opportunity, not a market-size sample.

| Step | Actual task | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Observe incumbent | Operator prepares a recent real update using their spreadsheet/importer/script; inspect past file and result | Trigger/date, frequency from records, payer/operator, minutes per step, failures and successes, current license/tool cost if volunteered |
| 2. Challenge the need | Ask them to use their existing selective-field/SKU workflow, WPMelon paste or an available incumbent demonstration/documentation | What is already adequate; setup they would lose; why pricing-only would fail; costs of adding a second tool |
| 3. Manual read-only preparation | Compare old/new supplier cost, exact SKU and timestamped Woo export; declared same currency/unit/tax basis; one multiplier selected by merchant | Match/duplicate/unmatched rows, original costs, proposed retail prices, uncertain inputs, decisions; record every assisted step |
| 4. Merchant review | Operator checks packet, changes/rejects proposals and resolves exceptions; describe core Preview/approval truthfully | Review minutes, corrections, missing capabilities and ignored outputs; no invented integrated Apply demo |
| 5. Repeat on next file | Operator voluntarily starts the same preparation at next natural cycle; reuse confirmed profile/baseline | Repeat initiation, help minutes, format drift, unresolved queue and comparison with actual incumbent time |
| 6. Buyer decision | Present explicit annual scope/price hypothesis, switching/setup burden and support exclusions | Budget owner's reason to buy or refuse, purchase process, incumbent spend; distinguish hypothetical answer from funded action |

The packet is a spreadsheet/review document, **not Pro**. Its output cannot currently be imported into Free as an implemented supplier adapter. Any staging demonstration uses the accepted Free artifact for its actual supported controls only, separately from manual proposals. Researcher-performed calculations must not be presented as product automation or savings available without human work.

## Questions that can reject the product

- Show the last supplier update. Which step was repeated, and why does the current tool not handle it? Show a successful run too.
- Does the file provide purchase cost, retail price or pack price? What exact currency/tax/unit basis is used? Who decides the multiplier and exceptions?
- Can updating only mapped prices, a saved import, or a spreadsheet lookup solve the problem today?
- Must stock, new products, XML/XLSX/PDF, FTP/API/ERP or multi-currency be included to make purchase rational? Record these as fit failures, not promised features.
- Which unresolved rows actually need follow-up? Does Free conflict → fresh Preview solve it? Who owns cross-job work?
- For promotions/incidents raised organically: must stored prices change, or would native dates/dynamic rules suffice? What happened and what did recovery cost?
- Who approves spend? What would be cancelled/replaced, and what price/scope would they reject? Would they return without the researcher prompting them?

## Evidence strength and acceptance

| Signal | Classification / strength |
|---|---|
| Praise, hypothetical “I'd pay,” forum posts, competitor price | PUBLIC SIGNAL or REPORTED interest; insufficient |
| Shown files/workflow, measured operator work and reproducible unresolved rows | OBSERVED in future experiment, if actually observed; stronger problem evidence |
| Operator initiates second use and finishes with limited assistance | OBSERVED repeat use, if it happens; does not prove renewal |
| Budget owner provides concrete procurement step and stated conditions | REPORTED buying intent; still no payment |
| Separately authorized, honestly scoped paid manual pilot | Actual payment evidence only after receipt; validates that offer, not automatically plugin WTP |
| Payment for a real product and later renewal | Stronger product/retention evidence; **NOT VERIFIED**, outside this experiment |

Advance to a Founder review only if an unmet recurring step survives the incumbent comparison, the narrow boundary is accepted, repeat use reduces measured operator work, and support effort fits [unit economics](FREE-PRO-PACKAGING-PRICING.md). Agree the merchant's acceptable correction/assistance burden before replay; do not invent a universal minutes-saved threshold afterward. No wrong identifier/basis proposal can count as a successful time saving.

A positive first case merits one independent buyer/workflow challenge, not a feature backlog. Evidence specificity governs sufficiency; no arbitrary interview quota or weighted score chooses a winner. A price commitment alone cannot replace repeat workflow evidence.

## Stop and report

Stop the candidate if incumbent use is adequate, recurrence is absent, price-only scope cannot replace meaningful work, unsupported formats/bases dominate, paid value is merely Free recovery, or support requires repeated bespoke cleanup. Ambiguous basis is refused rather than guessed. If no next cycle occurs within the window, report repeat use NOT VERIFIED; do not simulate it.

Proposed cap: eight Founder/researcher hours across up to four weeks; include preparation, onboarding, cleanup and follow-up in that budget. At expiry, deliver a short actual-evidence ledger with redacted examples, observed savings/corrections, no-switch cases, assistance economics, buyer decision and remaining uncertainty. Return for an explicit Founder commercial decision. No Pro tickets, code, licensing, publication or merchant commitments follow automatically.
