## Annual price hypotheses and solo-founder arithmetic

**HYPOTHESIS:** test USD 149/site/year for the narrow supplier job; USD 99 and 199 are sensitivity cases, not promised launch tiers. Only later, if agencies demonstrate the same job across sites, test USD 449/year for five production sites direct. No fleet feature implied. Marketplace would use the same single-site list fee. Avoid lifetime/unlimited support commitments during discovery.

Let `P` be collected annual price after discounts, excluding tax; `D` be refunds/other revenue deductions; `h` attributable support/onboarding hours; `w` an opportunity cost/hour; `M` annual fixed maintenance/acquisition cost; `N` paying accounts.

- Marketplace contribution = `0.70 × (P − D) − h × w − M/N`.
- Direct contribution = `P − D − actual processing/platform fees − h × w − M/N`.

**Illustration only:** zero deductions; direct fees assumed 5% inclusive (not a provider quote); `w = USD 25/hour`. Excludes taxes, development, acquisition and fixed maintenance. No customer count, retention or sales forecast is assumed.

| Annual P (USD) | Direct after assumed fee | Woo after 30% share | Direct after 3 support hours | Woo after 3 support hours |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 99 | 94.05 | 69.30 | 19.05 | -5.70 |
| 149 | 141.55 | 104.30 | 66.55 | 29.30 |
| 199 | 189.05 | 139.30 | 114.05 | 64.30 |

At USD 149, one support hour leaves USD 116.55 direct / 79.30 Woo before fixed costs. An **assumed** 100 annual fixed hours at USD 25 requires 22 / 32 such accounts to cover that USD 2,500; at three attributable support hours, 38 / 86. These are conditional break-even calculations, not acquisition targets or revenue predictions. More accounts also create more support. Renewal probability and second-year support remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

Merchant value must be measured as operator time saved per cycle × actual cycle frequency × their own time value, minus setup/switching cost. Incident risk cannot be priced from imagined losses. Proceed only if repeat value and support contribution survive the experiment without custom integration work disguised as plugin revenue.
