# Partner email drafts

Status: **not sent.** No recipient, no mailing list, and no follow-up sequence.
The founder sends one of these only to a person or company they already have
a reason to write to, or through a contact form that asks for tool notes.
A scraped list of hosts or agencies is out of scope.

Each email is useful if the reader never installs anything.

## 1. Agency that edits client catalogs

Subject: A catalog habit before the next sale price change

Hello,

I maintain WriteLeash, a free WordPress.org plugin for previewing WooCommerce
price edits. I am not asking for a partnership or a call.

The failure I keep seeing in public support threads is mechanical. Products-list
bulk edit writes on Update and documents no undo. A decrease of 100 percent
writes a sale price of zero, and the product can then be purchased for nothing.
Putting the same percentage back on does not restore the earlier price.

The habit that survives without any plugin: export ID, name, regular price, and
sale price before the change, edit a few rows, and compare the stored values
before touching the rest. If you already do that for clients, I would like to
hear where it still fails. If a one-page note of that sequence would be useful
for your team, I can send the note. If not, ignore this.

WriteLeash, if you ever look at it, only undoes prices it applied itself. It
does not undo a native bulk edit or a CSV import, and the public 0.2.2 release
does not change stock or lock staff.

https://wordpress.org/plugins/writeleash/

## 2. Host whose merchants ask about a bad import

Subject: When a WooCommerce import writes a zero sale price

Hello,

I maintain WriteLeash. This is one note, not a newsletter, and there is nothing
to buy.

A pattern in WooCommerce support is an import that leaves sale prices at 0.00,
so the shop gives the product away, or an import that stores the variation sale
while the parent product still displays the regular price. Those are different
repairs. Re-importing prices does not fix a stale parent range, and a backup
from before the file is what puts a zero back.

I am not asking you to recommend a plugin. If your support team already has a
checklist for "the sale price became zero" versus "the sale is in the variation
but not on the shop," I would learn from it. If you want the short version of
that split, say so and I will send only that.

The public plugin does not undo imports. I would rather your team keep using
the backup than install something that cannot reverse the file.

## 3. Freelancer who maintains one or two catalogs

Subject: Price history starts the day the log is turned on

Hello,

I maintain WriteLeash, a free WooCommerce price tool. Writing because catalog
cleanup is the work you already do. No list, and no ask to introduce me to
your clients.

WooCommerce does not keep a price log of its own. A history plugin, including
WriteLeash History, shows changes made after it was installed. It cannot
reconstruct last month's prices. The recoverable copy is the CSV or database
backup taken before the edit.

WriteLeash 0.2.2 can preview its own price job and undo an eligible price it
wrote. It will not lock an editor, block an AI plugin, or change stock. If
that boundary is still useful on a store you maintain, the listing is here:

https://wordpress.org/plugins/writeleash/

If you do not want notes like this, tell me and I will not write again.
