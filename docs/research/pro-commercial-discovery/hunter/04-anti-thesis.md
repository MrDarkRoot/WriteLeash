# Hunter — Anti-Thesis: The Case Against Building Pro

2026-10-13 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. **INFERRED: do not authorize a Pro build from this research.** The best current explanation is that WriteLeash Free supplies useful safe price editing while repeat commercial jobs are served by existing tools. This conclusion is conditional, not proof no merchant could ever pay.

## The strongest incumbent

**INFERRED:** WP All Import is the strongest overall supplier-workflow incumbent. It has documented repeat-feed machinery and a specific retained-user countercase (E11). This is an analyst judgment, not a market-share ranking. WPMelon is the closest local-plugin comparison; Pricebreeze is the most direct supplier-operations proposition. The shortlist cannot be won by claiming those products lack safety or exceptions.

No tested competitor shortcoming has been shown to justify migration. Confirmed documented limits, such as page-local paste or an Undo session bound, are investigation prompts; they become an opportunity only if a buyer repeatedly suffers from that limit and pays to remove it.

## Economics that remain unknown

**HYPOTHESIS:** support may dominate a solo developer's revenue if every customer brings different supplier identifiers, taxes, currencies, units, product types, cron behavior and third-party writers. A pricing-only product may force customers to retain their existing stock/catalog importer, reducing switching incentive. A self-hosted/local preference could matter, but no merchant in the sample established that preference as a buying reason.

Do not present speculative loss avoidance as ROI. Measure a merchant's actual residual task first. Net value must account for their incumbent setup, training, double-entry, support and integration cost as well as time saved. Neither forum product counts nor vendor-calculator defaults supply these quantities.

## What would change the anti-thesis

Founder-authorized discovery would need an identifiable buyer with successive real artifacts, a repeated costly residual task after using the configured incumbent, and a reason that Free cannot complete that distinct task. A truthful bounded demonstration must improve the actual procedure without merely moving work into a new UI. Paid commitment and subsequent real reuse should be recorded separately from interview interest; no arbitrary interview count guarantees validation.

Reject Opportunity 1 if its remaining work disappears after incumbent setup, is rare, primarily stock/catalog creation, or requires an unsupported generalized importer. Reject Opportunity 2 if contemporary campaign failures are absent or dynamic rules/native dates suffice. Defer all of Pro if neither survives. No decision requires degrading Free or inventing a separate paid Marketplace roadmap.
