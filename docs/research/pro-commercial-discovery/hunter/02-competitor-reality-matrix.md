## Public experience versus marketing

Merchant accounts are fully linked and dated in Document 1: E11 positive importer longevity; E12 specific paid-support obstacle; E13 positive Smart Manager workflow; E14 unusable generic complaint; E15 paid ELEX success; E16 balanced dynamic-price reviews; E18 legacy vendor-selected testimonials. ELEX's free listing also carries an October 17, 2024 update-access complaint and August 9, 2024 positive filtering/support review. Those are historical user reports, not a license-policy adjudication or present defect test.

**NOT VERIFIED:** independent experiences for new WPMelon restore/paste, Pricebreeze, Bulk Price Editor, Price and Stock Import, or the exact paid PW Scheduler. Do not fill gaps with legacy testimonials, install counts, demos or vendor example stores. Different free/paid editions and versions can explain conflicting reviews.

**INFERRED:** WP All Import is the strongest incumbent overall for the recurring supplier job because its documented scope and retained-user report cover established feed operations. WPMelon is the closest local-plugin challenge; Pricebreeze is the most directly overlapping proposition. Neither importer matching nor preview/history/Undo is a demonstrated unique WriteLeash advantage. A proposed gap must be tested against actual configured competitors, not their hero sections.
