## What changed in the evidence

**INFERRED:** There is public evidence for write amplification without AI: stock sync and metadata updates feed their own webhooks, including across stores. A small target set can receive unbounded repeat writes. That fits the original row-event counting idea more directly than a generic “AI approval” product.

But the same evidence documents successful fixes using origin markers and webhook filters. PostgreSQL operators also already have free missing-WHERE protection and a broader governance incumbent, Bytebase. A paid product must survive these alternatives. This round raises the specificity of the pain and lowers confidence in a standalone safety-plugin business.

## A sharper seatbelt hypothesis: authority must cover the effect chain

**HYPOTHESIS:** One certified automation integration could consume finite authority over a causally linked job, including repeated updates and descendant work. This refines opportunities 1/2 in Document 6, not a new product candidate. Its prospective payer is the agency/integration owner accountable for unattended writes; C1–C3 do not establish that payer will buy.

The unit being counted makes or breaks the value:

| Proposed bound | What it would constrain | What it still misses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinct object set | Unexpected SKU/account expansion | Ten permitted SKUs updated a million times. |
| Actual mutation events | Repeated/no-op writes, retry amplification | One allowed update setting a disastrous value; request overhead before mutation. |
| Specified transitions | Too many stocked products becoming unavailable | Other fields, uninstrumented routes, legitimate mass stock-out. |
| Trusted cumulative allowance | Multiple batches/retries/descendant executions sharing one approved scope | Writer minting fresh jobs, lost lineage, concurrent overspend, owner bypass. |

Illustrative allowance: 10 approved product IDs, price fields only, at most 30 update events across the task, no fresh allowance on webhook re-entry. These are invented test parameters, explicitly **not** merchant requirements. They do not describe existing WriteLeash functionality.

**INFERRED:** The difficult commercial question is not choosing a threshold. It is who can issue authority and whether every relevant mutation can be charged to it. A plugin that accepts a fresh job ID from any workflow can turn a feedback loop into unlimited “new” jobs. A task token cannot safely be copied between unrelated callers without trusted admission. Concurrency, expiry, retry accounting and fail-closed behavior require design and evidence. Do not promise this with a cooperative hook alone.

The current PostgreSQL V0's per-transaction budget cannot supply that lifetime guarantee. Woo's MySQL substrate is different. Use the exact [Document 6 boundaries](06-database-seatbelt-blast-radius.md#what-the-repository-actually-supports). Mutation control also does not automatically bound query scanning, latency, API charges or downstream external effects.

## Ranking update and falsification

**INFERRED:** Supplier-sync envelope remains the first operational lead; automation feedback is now a more concrete subcase than “AI does something bad.” PostgreSQL write leases remain original-thesis research with insufficient evidence of a job that truly needs broad SQL. Do not prioritize standalone loop protection: every direct loop example in this round either has a reported cheap fix or lacks confirmed recovery evidence. Bytebase is the strongest newly examined incumbent for production repair/governance.

Before a build decision, request permission for one redacted workflow export and its last normal/incident execution trace. Offline comparison should ask:

1. Does origin tagging/idempotency or an existing stop condition fully remove the repeated failure?
2. Does an excessive effect survive a native timeout, object filter and correct source-of-truth configuration?
3. Can each descendant write retain trusted lineage, and can another credential or plugin bypass coverage?
4. Does a merchant-selected allowance admit normal activity without stale stock or partially applied state?
5. Would the responsible agency buy a certified adapter, or reasonably expect its connector vendor to ship the fix?

**Recommendation: INVESTIGATE_FURTHER**, with explicit elimination: no generic AI firewall, no missing-WHERE-only paid product, no standalone loop plugin, no incident-recovery subscription justified by anecdotes. The residual test is a finite effect envelope for an integration whose necessary, recurring writes remain risky after competent configuration and cheap fixes. No confirmed customer, payment, useful threshold or complete enforcement topology has yet been obtained. No outreach was performed.
