## Explicit kill conditions

Do not build Pro when any essential condition persists after a bounded evidence attempt:

- No repeated payer-owned problem or no meaningful time/risk reduction versus the incumbent.
- Customers will pay only for capabilities already Free, higher safety limits, or withholding recovery.
- Attainable annual price cannot cover refunds/fees, support and acquisition at sustainable founder compensation.
- Mapping/setup must be custom consultancy each cycle; supplier formats or ERP/PIM/stock/product-creation dependencies are indispensable outside the supported scope.
- Native/dynamic/import/audit products satisfy the buyer and switching has no material benefit.
- Marketplace obligations exceed available maintenance/support capacity; this can kill the channel without killing direct sales.
- Renewal contribution is nonpositive, or measured retention requires acquisition replacement whose cost makes stable CM nonpositive.
- No feasible account count lies between economic break-even and founder capacity, or acquisition/build recovery cannot be funded inside the approved downside budget.

Formal hard economic rejection: at the **highest price real buyers accept**, `R − w×h − (1−q)×(c+w×o) ≤ 0`; more accounts then cannot fund fixed costs. Even if positive, reject the proposed business scope when `N_break_even > N_capacity` or no credible attainable demand supports N_break_even. Do not rescue the model by valuing founder time at zero or assuming infinite lifetime value.
