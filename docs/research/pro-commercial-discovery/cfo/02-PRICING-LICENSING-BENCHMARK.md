### Verification limitations and conflicts

- ELEX prices and currency were read from original first-party HTML: variations **3033/3034/3035** map single/5/25 sites to **79/169/399**; JSON-LD says USD. This avoids substituting old third-party price tables.
- Woo product text did not expose amounts to the text extractor. Original first-party JSON-LD UnitPriceSpecification supplied **69.00/99.00/129.00 USD**, `valueAddedTaxIncluded=false`; listings supply the one-year plan. No checkout was initiated.
- WP All Import repeats annual package labels alongside lifetime offers of both **$1,299 and $1,399**. Do not normalize these into a definitive current checkout price. Its unlimited-install positioning is strong price pressure on agency offers, regardless of unresolved checkout terms.
- WPMelon also advertises lifetime **$249/449/799** for 3/10/25 sites; Smart Manager **$549/599** for 1/5. These are competitor offers, not evidence that WriteLeash could finance lifetime support.
- Flycart's three-year section retains inconsistent “/year” and one-year entitlement text. The register uses the identifiable one-year offer only. Its broad guarantee headline is narrower in refund conditions; renewal refunds are excluded.
- UpdraftPlus pricing includes older “first 10 days” discretionary wording alongside 14-day guarantee text. The dedicated refund terms are the clearer current statement; do not promise unconditional refund eligibility from a headline.
- Pricebreeze remains unavailable live. Rounded monthly equivalents are not exact annual invoices; no invented annual total or refund policy. Recheck before any decision using its price.

## Commercial relevance, not feature-roadmap selection

| Alternative | Published workflow | Economic challenge to WriteLeash |
|---|---|---|
| WP All Import | Recurring mapped product imports and selective updates | An existing license/process can make switching worth little; price-only scope may lose to broader stock/product work. |
| WPMelon, Smart Manager, ELEX | Spreadsheet/bulk editing, variations, histories/undo; scheduling in Smart Manager/ELEX | A buyer can obtain many workflows at modest annual or agency prices. Preview and undo alone are not a paid differentiator. |
| Pricebreeze | Supplier files, matching, cost/margin review and changes | Direct overlap with #215. Service pricing is not proof of plugin WTP or acceptable WriteLeash support cost. |
| Bulk Price Editor | Preview/export, formulas, scheduled restore and history | A USD 69 Marketplace alternative makes a generic bulk-price paid tier difficult to justify. |
| PW, Dynamic Pricing, Flycart | Promotions/discount rules | [PW computes on the fly](https://woocommerce.com/document/pw-sales-scheduler-for-woocommerce/); avoiding stored-price restoration can be preferable to paying to manage it. |
| WP Activity Log | Audit, notifications, reports, external log options | Generic investigation may already be covered; universal attribution should not become a support promise. |
| UpdraftPlus | Backup, migration and restoration | Complements conditional price Undo, does not prove safe selective reversal of live commerce. Buyers may already pay for this broader recovery need. |

**CFO inference:** incumbents constrain the attainable price and acceptable onboarding effort. None of these prices establishes a WriteLeash buyer, payment commitment, renewal rate or customer-acquisition cost. Hunter/Sói should challenge the residual paid job against already-satisfied incumbent users.
