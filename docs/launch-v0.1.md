## Canonical product description

**Bulk price changes without blindly overwriting newer edits.** WriteLeash helps WooCommerce merchants Preview exact before-and-after prices before Apply, then change Regular Price or Sale Price across simple products and variations. Select by name, SKU or category, with up to 1,000 products in one job. Work continues in the background: leave and reopen wp-admin to review progress, and Resume interrupted work if needed. If a relevant price or checked product setting changed after Preview, WriteLeash preserves the newer edit and shows a conflict. Review what happened in History and Undo changes that are still safe to restore.

## GitHub Release description — v0.1.0

**Bulk price changes without blindly overwriting newer edits.**

WriteLeash is a WooCommerce bulk-pricing plugin for store owners, catalog operators, agencies and freelancers.

- Preview exact before-and-after prices before Apply.
- Change Regular Price or Sale Price for simple products and variations; select by name, SKU or category.
- Handle up to 1,000 products in one job, with background work and Resume if interrupted.
- Preserve relevant edits made after Preview and show conflicts for review.
- Review History and Undo changes that are still safe to restore.

See the [plugin listing and supported scope](../wordpress/writeleash/readme.txt) before installing. Tell us what confused you during a real pricing task through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/MrDarkRoot/WriteLeash/issues), without customer data or secrets.
